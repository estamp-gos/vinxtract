About VinXtract

The National Motor Vehicle Title Information System (VinXtract) is an electronic system that contains information on certain automobiles titled in the United States. VinXtract is intended to serve as a reliable source of title and brand history for automobiles, but it does not contain detailed information regarding a vehicle's repair history.

All states, insurance companies, and junk and salvage yards are required by federal law to regularly report information to VinXtract. However, VinXtract does not contain information on all motor vehicles in the United States because some states are not yet providing their vehicle data to the system. Currently, data is provided in a variety of time frames; while some states report in real-time, other states send updates less frequently, such as once every 24 hours.

Information on previous, significant vehicle damage may not be included in the system if the vehicle was never determined by an insurance company (or other appropriate entity) to be a "total loss" or branded by a state.
